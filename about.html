About

My interests sit at the intersection of mechanical design, vehicle systems, simulation and manufacturing.

I enjoy taking systems from requirements and rough concepts through CAD, analysis, manufacturing and physical validation.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Purdue University, graduating in 2028. So far that's meant leading the front axle and spindle assembly on Purdue ASME Racing's Grand Prix kart, designing a tube-launched UAV airframe as an intern at Bravecore in Kerala, and leading a can crusher from customer need to delivery for Lafayette Habitat for Humanity.
